Overlay · transparent always-on-top window · Windowed Fullscreen only

Glanceable, then gone.

One panel at a time, readable in under a second, never covering the minimap, kill feed, ability bar or crosshair. Shown over an abstract game scene with the HUD zones it must avoid. The overlay follows the app theme; its glass is 88% opaque by default so text stays readable even over a white sky.

Rules

Behaviour

• Click-through at all times except in edit mode. Never takes keyboard focus from the game.
• Default anchor: top right, 24 px from the edges, below the scoreboard strip. Snaps to 9 anchors or free position; saved per monitor resolution.
• Panels cycle with Ctrl Shift Tab; dots in the header show which one. Show/hide with Ctrl Shift H.
• Motion: fade + 8 px slide, 200 ms in, 120 ms out. Numbers never animate while you're in a round.
• Scale: S 0.85×, M 1×, L 1.2× (and per-DPI). Glass opacity 75–100%, default 88% (readable over a white sky in every theme). It changes only the panel background; below 88% secondary text switches to --text-2.
• Never during agent select: nothing about other players. Comp Advisor only uses picks you enter yourself.
• Live match (opt-in, warning accepted): after the match starts: a buy-phase panel in round 1, a rail while Tab is held, and a full table on Ctrl Shift L. Private profiles and hidden names say Private; hidden names are never looked up.
