Postplant design system · v1 · 2026-11-11

Built to be
read in a glance.

Everything needed to build Postplant without making design decisions later: brand, six themes, type, tokens, every component and state, every app screen, and the in-game overlay. Use the toolbar to switch theme, font and colour-blind mode; every page follows along.

Calm by default

Neutral surfaces; colour only means something (win, loss, side, role, accent action).

Numbers first

Big tabular numbers in the display face; labels small and quiet.

Honest data

Every stat can show its sample size, date and source. Missing data is hidden, never faked.

Out of the way

The overlay is glass, small, and silent unless something changed.

02 · Themes

Six themes, three dark, three light

Ink is the default. Each theme has the same tokens, so components never hard-code a colour. Every text colour, including the faintest (--text-3), reaches 4.5:1 on every background and surface step of its theme, checked on every element of these pages. Page, sidebar and cards are clearly separate steps; light themes also lift cards with a soft shadow (--card-shadow). "Follow Windows" picks Ink or Paper automatically. Click a card to preview it.

03 · Typography

Satoshi for words, Big Shoulders for numbers

UI text uses Satoshi by default. Users can switch to General Sans, Geist, Inter or Atkinson Hyperlegible in Settings → Appearance. Headline numbers and the overlay's big results use Big Shoulders Display; data and keys use JetBrains Mono. All numbers use tabular figures.

04 · Space & shape

4 px grid, soft corners, shallow depth

Spacing steps 4–64 px. Controls are 28 / 36 / 44 px tall. Radius grows with size: 6 small controls, 10 controls, 14 list rows, 20 cards and modals. Elevation is used only for things that float (menus, modals, toasts, overlay).

05 · Motion

Quick, springy, and never in the way

Hover 120 ms, small changes 200 ms, panels 320 ms, modals and page changes 520 ms. Entering uses ease-out; toggles use a light spring. The overlay never animates while idle; it only animates when data changes. Game-safe rules: only transform and opacity animate; nothing loops while VALORANT runs (data-perf="game" stops shimmer, blur and timers); the overlay has no blur and no hover (it is click-through), just a 200 ms entrance; hidden windows stop rendering. Everything respects "reduce motion" in Windows.

06 · Icons

Lucide, 2 px stroke

Use Lucide (ISC licence) at 16 px in controls and 20 px in headers, stroke 2, colour from text tokens. Agent, map and rank art is loaded at runtime from valorant-api.com and never committed to the repo; the designs use role-tinted monograms in its place.

07 · Data colours

Colour carries meaning, and never alone

Win/loss always come with W/L letters or +/−; sides come with ATK/DEF labels; roles with a diamond and name. Colour-blind mode swaps win/loss to blue/orange (toolbar "CVD"). Ranks use a diamond "gem" tinted by tier (the official rank icon replaces it at runtime).

08 · Writing

Talk like a good duo

Short, plain, specific. Facts before opinions. Never blame the player, never shout.

Do
  • "2 losses in a row. Take a breather?"
  • "Not enough data on Corrode yet (n = 41)."
  • "Couldn't reach HenrikDev. Showing saved data."
  • "Delete 214 matches? This can't be undone."
Don't
  • "You're tilting!!"
  • "Omen is the best agent." (no source, no sample)
  • "Error 429."
  • "Are you sure?" (says nothing about what happens)
